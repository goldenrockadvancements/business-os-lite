Welcome to **Business OS Lite**, the free edition of [Business OS](https://goldenrockadvancements.com/vaults/business-os/). It keeps your **clients** and **tasks** in one place, and the [[Home]] page shows what needs you today.

## Set up in 3 minutes
1. Look at the sample clients in `01 Clients`. They're fictional and tagged `#sample`.
2. Add a real client: `Ctrl/Cmd + N`, name it after the company, type `/` > *Insert template* > **Client**. Fill in *status*, *next step* and *next date*.
3. Add a task with the **Task** template and put the client in its *client* field (type `[[` to pick it).
4. Open [[Home]]: your task and follow-up show up there.
5. Clear the samples when you're ready: [[Start fresh]].

The views are Obsidian **Bases** (built in, no plugins). To change one, see the [[Bases cheat sheet]].
